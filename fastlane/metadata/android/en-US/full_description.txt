RUSerBox is a Linux, Unix and Windows server monitoring and management tool, written in Rust and drawn with Vulkan.

Key features:
• Status charts for CPU, memory, disks, network, sensors and GPUs
• An SSH terminal (Zellij sessions included) and SFTP
• Docker and Podman containers, processes, systemd services and snippets
• Home-screen widgets and alerts through the ServerBox Monitor
• Gruvbox themes and 17 languages

Requires a 64-bit ARM device with Android 9 or newer and Vulkan 1.1.
