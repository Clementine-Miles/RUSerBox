ServerBox (RUSerBox build) is a Linux server administration and terminal app for Android.

Key features:
• Live and historical charts for CPU, memory, disks, network, sensors and GPUs
• SSH terminal with Zellij sessions, SFTP/SCP file manager, jump hosts and port forwards
• Docker/Podman, processes, systemd, users, firewall, libvirt and Proxmox VE
• A local shell and PRoot Linux userlands on the device
• Built-in Gruvbox themes, biometric lock and encrypted backups

Special thanks to dartssh2 and xterm.dart.
