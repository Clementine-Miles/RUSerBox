---
title: Appearance
description: Choose a Gruvbox palette, icon family, and terminal font.
---

Open **Settings → App → Appearance** to select **Gruvbox Material** or **Gruvbox**. Both palettes support light and dark mode. Choose **Auto** to follow Android's system setting. The Classic and MingCute icon families are available independently of the palette.

Open **Settings → Terminal** to choose a font, font size, line height, and terminal brightness. Hack Nerd Font is the default. JetBrains Mono, Fira Code, Iosevka, and Cascadia Code are bundled for offline use. See **Settings → About → License** for their notices.

The palette colors are based on [Gruvbox Material](https://github.com/sainnhe/gruvbox-material) and [Gruvbox](https://github.com/morhetz/gruvbox).
